# Phase change
- Process where a substance changes from one state to another
- Happens when energy (heat mostly) is added/removed or when pressure changes

| Phase Change   | Process     |
| -------------- | ----------- |
| solid > liquid | melting     |
| liquid>solid   | freezing    |
| liquid>gas     | boiling     |
| gas>liquid     | condensing  |
| solid>gas      | sublimation |
| gas>solid      | deposition  |
# Heat
- Heat (Q) is transfer of thermal energy
- measured in J
- Always flows from hot to cold
- [[11.11.26 Conservation of energy]]

# Heat flow
- Fast moving particles collide with slow particles and increase their velocity, kinetic energy, and temp
# Methods of heat transfer
- suggests that energy is transferred from warm body to cooler body
	- Conduction
	- Convection
	- Radiation

> [!NOTE] Conduction
> Occurs between objects in direct contact
> High temp -> High Avg kinetic energy
> Fast moving particles collide with slow and transfer heat

> [!NOTE] Convection
> Occurs when fluids (liquids or gas) move around due to temp differences
> Hot air rises
> Cold air sinks

> [!NOTE] Radiation
> Energy transferred as electromagnetic waves such as visible light and infrared
> Radiation can travel through a vacuum
