# Conductors and Insulators
Conductor
- Material through which [[10.4.26 Energy and Energy transformations]] can be easily transfered as heat
	- [[11.19.26 Heat vs Temperature]]
Insulator
- Material that transfers energy poorly

# Thermal Conductivity
(K): Measures how fast heat flows through a material across a temperature gradient
Mentioned [[9.24.26 Metallic Bonding]] [[10.4.26 Bonding Type and Properties]]
# Calculating
$$Q/t=kA(T/x)$$

Q -> energy transfered thru material
t -> time to transfer thermal energy
Q/t ->Power transferred (W)
k -> thermal conductivity
A -> cross section area ($m^2$)
T -> change in temp
	$T=T_2-T_1$
x -> material thickness (length (m))
