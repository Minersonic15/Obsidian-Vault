# Shakespear motivations
- Shakespeare wanted to gain favour with James I as his ability to continue as a playwright depended on monarch's endorsement
- Shakespeare chose to make the witches the catalyst for evil and unnatural actions of the play to appeal to James as cautionary tale to overthrow
- Banquo, ancestor of King is uncorrupted
