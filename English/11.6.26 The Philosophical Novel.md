# Ovid's Metamorphosis
- Ovid was a Roman poet who wrote the poem The Metamorphosis
- Ovid's work explores various myths within the Greco-Roman pantheistic beliefs
- As title suggests, it focuses on the transformation of each myth and how transformation changes the next series of events
- "The current age has only been made present because of past transformations, and present transformations will dictate the future"
# How effect kafka
- The metamorphosis uses the main idea from Ovid's change
- Gregor's transformation into an insect will cause other characters to transform
- One question Gregor examines is "What is the nature of transformation" or "What are the consequences of change1"
