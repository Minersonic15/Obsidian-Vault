# Biography
- Born July 3, 1883 in Prague Austria-hungary
	- now Czechia
- Eldest of Six children to middle-class family in Bohemia
- Initially studied Chemistry
- After university, Kafka worked many jobs, including unpaid legal work
- Despite his indifference to these jobs, he was promoted several times.

- Suffered from number of ailments during his life, including
	- Tuberculosis
	- Depression
	- Social anxiety
	- migraness
	- insomnia
- At sanitorium, his Tuberculosis worsened and he could not eat due to pain
- Believed to have died June 3, 1924
- Fate mirrors that of Gregor in The Metamorphosis and the protag in The Hunger Artist [[11.6.26 The Philosophical Novel]]
# Style

# Surrealism
- Develops parallel to kafka's writing
- Kafka's writing shows evidence of many concepts and stylistic elements
- Surrealism is art of surprise, unexpected juxtapositions, and non sequiters, encompassing sub-cultural expressions 

- Surrealists defined their movement in their manifesto
	- "Psychic **Automatism** in its pure state, by which one proposes to express -- verbally, by means of the written word, or in any other manner -- the actual functioning of thought. Dictated by the thought, i the absence of any control exercised by **reason** exempt from any aesthetic or moral connection"
