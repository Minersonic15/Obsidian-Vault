# Types of Acceleration
- Speeding Up
- Slowing down
- change direction


> [!NOTE] Slowing or speeding
> When acceleration is in same direction as velocity, object is **speeding up**
> When acceleration is in opposite dir as velocity, object is **slowing down**

# Displacement vs Time
- a curve on S vs T graph describes acceleration
- For curve, estimate slop eusing a tangent line
- slope of tangent is instant velocity
