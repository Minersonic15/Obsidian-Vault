# Rounding
- Round down if below 5, up if above 5
	- 0.7432 GO TO 0.74
	- 0.3571 go to 0.36
- Round up if digit before 5 is odd
	- 0.275 go to 0.28 i guess
- round down if digit before 5 is even
	- 0.265 gets rounded to 0.26 i guess or something

Why use 5 rule?
	- Automatically rounding up skews data in one direction
