- Designed for triangles that are non-right angle
- Sine Law:
	- $sinA/a = sinB/b = sinC/c$
- Cosine Law:
	- $a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc*cosA$
	- $b^2=a^2+c^2-2ac*cosB$
	- $c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab*cosC$
