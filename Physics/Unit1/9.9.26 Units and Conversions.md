Two Types of Observations:

| Quantitative    | How Many/How Much | eg. 13 apples on table |
| --------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Qualitative** | Description       | eg. apple is red       |
# Qualitative Units
- physicists deal with physical quantities which are those things that are measurable
	- Mass
	- Length
	- Time
	- etc
- Fundamental quantities are so basic that other quantities are expressed in terms of them
# International System of Units
 Developed from metric system
 Both units and prefixes
