# Normal force
why we don't get pulled straight down to centre of earth from gravity
- Normal force
- Force provided by a surface to counter gravity

# Types of forces
Contact forces:
- Touching object
Applied Force
- Force used to help move an object
Force of Friction
- Force that opposes sliding motion between surfaces
Normal force
- Force a surface exerts on an object to support it
	-  perpendicular to surface
Tension
- Pull exerted by string rope or cable
At-A-Distance Forces:
- Not touching objetc
Gravitational Force
- Attraction between two objects, generally earth and object
Electrostatic force
- attraction or repulsion between 2 charged forces
Magnetic force
- force of attraction or repulsion between electrically charged particles

- Tension can only be a pull, never a push
- Friction tries to oppose motion
	- parallel to contact surface
- Normal is  perpendicular to contact surface
- Friction+normal are **Surface contact forces**
# Free body diagram
Used to show magnitude and direction of all forces acting on an object
- Dot or box to represent object
- arrows to represent forces
	- Arrows point outward from object
	- length of arrow is size of force
	- forces must be labelled
	- Do not include forces that object exerts on other objects
