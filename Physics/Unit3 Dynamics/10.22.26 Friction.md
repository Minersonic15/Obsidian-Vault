[[10.13.26 Intro to forces]]

# Advantages
- Enables Movement:
	- People can walk without slipping
- Stops Vehicles
	- Allows vehicles to break and tires grip road
- Mechanical function
	- Transfer motion via belts and gears
# Disadvantages
- Wear and tear
	- Grinds down moving parts, tires, etc
- Increased fuel consumption
	- Requires vehicles to burn more fuel to overcome frictional resistance
- Noise and Vibration
	- Irritating sound
# How Calculate
$F_f= u*F_n$
Friction= Coefficient of Friction* Normal Reaction Force

$Us*Fn$ calculates maximum static friction
- Amount of force to overcome to start moving a stationary object
