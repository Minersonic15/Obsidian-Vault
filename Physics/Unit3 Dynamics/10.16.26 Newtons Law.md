# First Law
- Newtons first law is **Law of Inertia**
- if all forces acting on body are balanced, object will not change speed or direction
- Object at rest stays at rest, object in motion stays in motion

# Facts about Net force
[[10.15.26 Net Force]]
- Object may be at rest or moving at a constant velocity
- Acceleration only occurs when:
- $$Fnet > 0orFnet<0$$
# Second Law
- If there is an unbalanced force acting on an object, it will accelerate in the direction of the net force
- Mass resists acceleration
- More mass = more **Inertia**
- $Force=mass*acceleration$
	- $Fnet=m*a$
	- $N =kg*m$
