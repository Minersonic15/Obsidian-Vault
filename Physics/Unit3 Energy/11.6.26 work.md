- When energy is added or removed from a system, that is called **work**
- Work is done when a force is applied to an object through a distance
# Calculations
Work* = Force x Displacement
- [[10.13.26 Intro to forces]] [[9.29.26 Intro to motion]]
$$W=F*S$$
Units:
$$[J]=[N]*[M]$$
# Necessities for work
- Must be a force
- Must be displacement
- Direction
	- can be pos or neg
	
> [!NOTE] Positive Work
> Force in the same direction as displacement
> Object gains energy

> [!NOTE] Negative Work
> Force in the opposite direction of displacement
> Object looses energy

# Zero Work
1. When the direction of objects displacement is exactly 90cdegrees to applied force
2. When there is a force but no displacement
3. When no force is applied but object is being displaced
