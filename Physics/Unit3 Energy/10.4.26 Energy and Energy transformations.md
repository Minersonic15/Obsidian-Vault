# What is energy
- the ability to cause change or movement, or accomplish a task
-  transferred from one object to another when change occurs
- comes in many interchangeable forms
- can be stored and used later
- Always conserved and cannot be created nor destroyed

# Einstein
- Einstein theorized that matter and energy are two forms of the same thing.
	- Matter + Energy interchangeable
- He defined relationship by quantifying the amount of energy stored in an object at rest due to mass
	- $E=mc^2$
		- E = energy in Joules
		- m = mass in kg
		- c = speed of light
			- $3.0*10^8m/s$

# Potential energy
- Stored energy due to an objects state
	- Energy something has the potential to do
- Types:
	- **Chemical**: Energy stored within the molecules of substance
	- **Nuclear**: Energy stored within the nucleus of atom
	- **Gravitational**: stored energy of object due to its height above a surface
	- **Elastic Potential**: energy stored by bending or compressing an elastic material
	- **Electric Potential**: Stored energy of charged object due to position and charge
	- **Magnetic Potential**: Stored energy of magnetized objects
# Kinetic energy
- Energy of motion
- Types
	- **Bulk Kinetic**: energy due to motion of an entire object
	- **Sound:** Energy transmitted from molecule to molecule with z1a medium
	- **Thermal**: Energy an object has due to motion of molecules within a substance
	- **Radiant**: Energy transmitted via electromagnetic waves such as radio, light, x-rays 
		- [[Unit 1 study guide]] H-emission!!
	- **Electric**: Energy associated with moving electric charges
# Energy transformations
- Energy can be transformed from one form to another
	- Most household devices turn electrical energy into another form
	- e.g microwave oven:
		- Electrical energy -> radiant energy, sound energy, kinetic energy
