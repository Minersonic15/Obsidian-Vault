# Kinetic Energy
[[10.4.26 Energy and Energy transformations]]
- energy of motion Ek
- Bigger speed V, bigger Ek
- Bigger mass, bigger Ek
$$E_k = 1/2mv^2$$
- Unit for Ek is (kg * m/s)m
	- Mass multiplied by an acceleration, which is a Newton
- Ek measured in Newtons * mass, which are **Joules(J)**
- Many use K or KE for kinetic energy

- Work and Kinetic Energy have same units

# Gravitational Potential Energy
- Energy due to position of a weight is gravitational Potential energy
- $$e_p=mg[delta]h$$
- e.g.
	- Bowling ball raised to height [delta]h, and then let go. it fall and speed up, gaining kinetic energy.
	- Lift constitutes work against gravity (weight of ball) so
	- $W=F_sCos0$
