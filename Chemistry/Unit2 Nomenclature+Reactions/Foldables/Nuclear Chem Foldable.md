# Isotopes
- Naturally occurring variations of an element
- Same num of protons and electrons
- Different num of neutrons and a diff atomic mass
# Average atomic mass
weighted average that reflects both mass of element and proportions it naturally occurs
- $$atomic mass = (massOfIsotope * relativeAbundance) + (massOfIsotope * relativeAbundance)$$
# Half life
- Time taken for radioactivity of a isotope to fall to half its original value

# Types of particles

| Type           | Symbol                     |
| -------------- | -------------------------- |
| Alpha Particle | 4/2He or 4/2(alpha symbol) |
| Beta particle  | 0/-1e or 0/-1B             |
| Positron       | 0/+1e or 0/+1B             |
| Gamma ray      | 0/0Y                       |
Alpha particles
- Paper, clothing, skin
Beta
- Heavy clothing, wood
Gamma
- Lead, concrete
# Alpha decay
- Loss of one alpha particle
- produces 4/2He
# Beta Decay
- loss of beta particle
- produces 0/-1e
# Nuclear Fission
- Splitting of heavy nucleus into two or more fragments
- Usually result of impact of a neutron on the nucleus
# Nuclear Fusion
- Reaction between a light atomic nucleus where heavier nucleus is formed with release of energy
