# Decomposition
- Compound breaks down into elements or other compounds
	- AB -> A+B
- Carbonates decompose to carbon dioxide and metal oxide
	- [metal]CO3 -> CO2 + [M]O
- Chlorates decompose to oxygen and metal chloride
	- [Metal]ClO3 -> O2+Cl[M]
- Sulfites also go to sulfur dioxide and metal oxide

# Combustion
- Reaction of hydrocarbon or element with oxygen
- Accompanied by production of heat+light

Types:
- Metal+Oxygen
	- Produces metal oxide (synthesis
	- [Metal] + oxygen ->
- Complete combustion
	- Enough oxygent to completely react the hydrocarbon
	- Always produce carbon dioxide + water
	- **CH4(g)+2O2(g) -> CO2 + 2H2O**
	- Balanced:
		 CH4(g)+2O2(g) -> CO2 + 2H2O
	- 1:2
- Incomplete combustion
	- Insufficient oxygen to react hydrocarbon
	- Always prpduce carbon dioxide, water, carbon monoxide, carbon (soot)
	- **CH4(g) +O2(g) -> CO2 + H2O +CO + C**
	- Balanced:
		 4CH4(g) +6O2(g) -> CO2 + 8H2O +2 CO + C
	- 1:1.5
- ONLY reactions that can be balanced with fraction

# Double Displacement
- Exchange of cations or anions between 2 compounds
- AB+CD -> AD+CB
- Two types:
> [!NOTE] No Reaction
> No Visible Chem change 
> (All products aqueous)

> [!NOTE] Precipitate Formation
>Insoluble product formed 
>(Solubility guidelines) [[10.14.26 Balancing]]

> [!NOTE] Gas Formation
> Secondary decomposition reaction occurs
> (carbonates, sulfites, chlorates)
> 3 products

> [!NOTE] Neutralization
>  Reaction between acid and base to form salt and ater
>  Results in neutral product
>  pH=7
> Bases and acids normally aqueous
