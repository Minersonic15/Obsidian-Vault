[[10.16.26 Synthesis and Decomposition reactions]]
# Synthesis
- 2 Reactants (elements/compounds) to form one products

| Reactants               | Product         |
| ----------------------- | --------------- |
| Metal+Non-metal         | Ionic           |
| Metal+Oxy               | Metal oxide     |
| Metal oxide + water     | base            |
| Non-metal + oxygen      | Non-metal oxide |
| non-metal oxide + Water | Acid            |
Remember to [[10.14.26 Balancing]] equations

# Decomposition
- Compound breaks down into elements or other compounds
	- AB -> A+B
- Carbonates decompose to carbon dioxide and metal oxide
	- [metal]CO3 -> CO2 + [M]O
- Chlorates decompose to oxygen and metal chloride
	- [Metal]ClO3 -> O2+Cl[M]
