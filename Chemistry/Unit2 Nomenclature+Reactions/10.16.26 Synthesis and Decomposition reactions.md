# Synthesis reaction
- In a synthesis reaction, two reactants combine to form a larger or more complex product
- For this, Reactants must collide, break existing bonds and form new bonds.
- e.g.
	- A+B -> AB

Steps:
1. Identify reactants
	1. Mg $O_2$
2. Identify type of reaction. These reactants fit pattern of synthesis
	1. A +B -> AB
	2. Mg+O2 -> MgO2
3. Use ionic charges of the reactants to predict the formula of the product. Reduce to simplest ratios if necessary
	1. Ionic charges: Mg2+, O2-
	2. Product: Mg2O2
	3. Skeleton equation: Mg+O2-> MgO
4. Balance chem equation and write balanced equation using state symbols [[10.14.26 Balancing]]
	1. 2Mg(s) +O2(g) -> 2MgO(s)

- We can apply the pattern of synthesis reactions to Molecular compounds [[10.9.26 Covalent Compounds]]
- Synth reactions with Hydrogen are more predictable, but without it can be difficult. Product depends on the reaction conditions
# Decomposition Reaction
Many explosions are decomposition reactions
- Simple ionic compounds can be made to decompose into their elements
- e.g
	- 2KCl(l) -electricity-> 2K(s) + Cl2(g)
- Using Polyatomic ions is different.
	- 2KClO2(s) -heat-> 2K(s) + Cl2(g)
- Some produce only compounds.
	- CaCO produces a gas
	- CaCO3(s) -heat-> CaO(s) + CO2(g)
