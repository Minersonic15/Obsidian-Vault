# Double Displacement
- No Reaction
	- No Visible CHem change
	- All aqueous
- Precipitate formation
	- insoluble product formed
	- soluble + aqueous
- Gas Formation
	- Secondary decomposition reaction occurs 
	- carbonates, sulfites, chlorates
- Neutralization
	- Reaction between acid and base to form salt and water
	- neutral (pH = 7)
	- Base+Acid (aqueous)
# Combustion
- Complete combustion
	- Enough oxygen to completely react the hydrocarbon
	- 1:2 CH to Oxygen
	- Always produce CO2 + H2O
- Incomplete Combustion
	- Not enough oxygen to completely react hydrocarbon
	- 1:1.5
	- Always produce CO2,  H2O, CO, C
- These are only reactions that can be balanced with fractions
