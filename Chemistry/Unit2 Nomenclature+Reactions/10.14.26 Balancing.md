# Chemical Equations
Reactant - Substance undergo reaction
Product - Formed during reaction
Reactant -> product

# Skeleton Equation
- Must include states (l), (s) (aq), (g)

# Conservation of mass
Matter neither created nor destroyed
- Mass of product = total mass of reactants


# Solubility rules

| Compound type       | Solubility in water | Exceptions                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Group 1 ions + NH4+ | Always soluble      | -                               |
| No3-, ClO4-,C2H3O2- | Always              | Ag+, Pb2+, Hg2+, Ca2+           |
| Cl-, Br-, I-        | Solublae            | Ba2+, Pb2+, Hg2+, Ca2+          |
| CO3^2-, PO4^3-      | Insoluble           | Group 1, NH4+, Ba2+, Sr2+, Ca2+ |
| OH-, S2-            | Insoluble           | Group 1, NH4+, Ba2+, Sr2+, Ca2+ |
