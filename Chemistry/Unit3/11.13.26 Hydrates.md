- A crystalized ionic compound [[9.28.26 Chemical Bonding]] from a water solution with water molecules incorporated into the structure, forming a hydrate
- Have specific num of water molecules chemically bonded to each formula unit
- Weak bond between water molecules and crystal

# Anhydrate
- No water molecules incorporated.
- evaporated out
$$Degree of Hydration=WaterMole/AnhydrateMole$$
Anhydrate never bigger than water
- When calculating empirical formula [[11.5.26 Percent composition]]
	- Anhydrate always 1, not water.
