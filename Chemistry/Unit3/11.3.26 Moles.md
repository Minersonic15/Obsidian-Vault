# Molar Mass
1 u = $1.66*10^-24$ g/u
- The mass of 1 mole of an element or compound
- One mole of an element has a mass expressed in grams, numerically equivalent of the elements average atomic mass
	- 1 atomic mass x Avogadro's Num = 1 gram
	- [[10.27.26 Isotopes + atomic mass]]
- Unit g/mol
	- #ib g mol^-1

**NEVER round molar mass**
When calculating, do it at least twice, just in case is wrong
- Molar mass wrong, everything else wrong
