[[11.6.26 Determining chemical formula from percentage composition]]
- Use empirical formula [[11.5.26 Percent composition]] and its molar mass to determine molecular formula
- Indicates the num of atoms of each element in a compound
- Same or different from empirical formula
# Steps
1. Find empirical formula [[11.5.26 Percent composition]]
2. Find molar mass of Empirical
3. Compare Mass of Empirical to Mass provided
	- $MolecularMass/EmpiricalMass$
4. Multiply subscripts of empirical by this factor7
