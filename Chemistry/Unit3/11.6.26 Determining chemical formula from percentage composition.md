# Empirical Formula to Chemical Formula
1. Divide the relative mass of the chem ([[11.3.26 Moles]]) by the empirical formula mass
2. Multiply the formula by the number from step 1

e.g.
	AlCl3
	empirical mass: 267
	relative mass: 133.33
	$267/133.33 = 2$
	**Al2Cl6**
