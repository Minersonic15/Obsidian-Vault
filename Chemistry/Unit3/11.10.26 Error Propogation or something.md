# Random Equipment Errors
- Precision: Errors due to instrument
- e.g.
	- Reading scales of a balance, graduated cylinder, thermometer
- Uncertainties:
	- **Errors resulted of tools**
Calculate uncertainties:
- Analog
	- Half of smallest division on scale, divided 2
		- [[11.2.26 Uncertainties and Errors]]\
- Digital
	- Based on num of decimal places
		- Balance measures to 3DP, uncertainty of +/-0.001 
# Expressing Uncertainties
- Absolute
	- Actual measurement
- Percentage
	- $PercentError=Absolute/Measurement * 100$
# Adding + Subtracting Uncertainties
- Sum of Absolute Errors
- Rounded to correct num of [[9.10.26 Significant Figures and Scientific Notation]]
# Multiplying + Dividing Uncertainties
- Sum of Percentage errors
- Rounded to correct Sig Figs of uncertainty
- Steps:
	1. Convert absolute to percentage Uncertainties
	2. Add % uncertainties
	3. Convert Percentage to Absolute
		- $Absolute = (Percent *Measurement/100)$
# Tips
- Do not round until final answer
- Round to correct number of SD from question
	- Diff # SD for answer vs error

# Systematic (Experimental)Error
- Due to incorrect use of equipment / poor design
- Measure of accuracy
- Literature Values found in data tables or textbooks
	- Official data of results
$$ErrorPercent = ((Literature-Experimental)/Literature)*100$$
[[9.15.26 Types of Errors, Accuracy and Precision]]
- Experimental error < Equipment error:
	- Results within acceptable error of equipment
# Types of Systematic Errors
- Personal
	- Ignorance, carelessness, physical limitations, Repeat.
- Instrumental
	- Imperfections with lab equipment (uncertainties)
- Method
	- Only manipulated independant variable
	- More variables = more error
