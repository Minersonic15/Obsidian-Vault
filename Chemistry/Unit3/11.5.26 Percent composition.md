# Percent Composition
- Determined based on the percentage mass
- Analyse a sample of a compound and use the molar mass to determine the % of each element by mass
	- [[11.3.26 Moles]]
$$percentX = (MolarMassX/MolarMassOfCompound)*100$$
e.g:
	Al (NO3)3
	Al=26.98
	N=42.04
	O= 144
	Total Mass = 213.02
	Al%= 12.67%
	N%= 19.74%
	O%= 67.6%

# Empirical Formula
- Simplest whole-num ratio of each element present in a compound
	- e.g:
		- C6H12O6
		- CH2O
- Given results of a percentage composition, you can find the Empirical Formula
Steps:
1. Convert % to mass (100g)
2. Find moles of each element [[11.3.26 Moles]]
	- $n=m/M_r$
3. Divide each element by the lowest num of moles to develop simple mole ratio
4. Mole ratios=subscripts of formula
	- If not whole, multiply everything so everything whole!!!
		- .5 X2
		- .9 round up
		- .1 round down  
![[Empirical Formula.png]]
E.g
	mass:
		26.52g chromium
		24.52g sulfur
		48.96g oxygen
	moles:
		Cr=0.51 moles
		S=0.764577486 moles
		oxygen = 3.06 moles
	Ratio:
		Cr: 1
		S: 1.499171543
		O: 6
	Formula:
		$CrS_1.5_6$ NO WRONG BAD!!!
		$Cr_2S_3O_12$ good, good
		$Cr_2(SO_4)_3$ also works
