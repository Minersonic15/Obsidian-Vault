

| Technique                   | Description                                                                                      | Components Removed                             | Components Remaining                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Filtation                   | Mixture poured through filter paper                                                              | Insoluble solid (residue)                      | Filtrate (liquid, solution)                                                                                                   |
| Evaporation                 | Separate mixture which has solute dissolved in solvent by heating                                | Solute                                         | Solvent (water)                                                                                                               |
| Solvation (crystallization) | Separation of heterogeneous mixture of two solids separation based on solubility differences     | Insoluble solid separated by filtration        | Soluble substance separated by evaporation                                                                                    |
| Distillation                | Mixture heated until one or more components vaporised by diff in volatility (BP)                 | Liquid with lower BP evaporates first          | Distillate (vapour condenses back to liquid)                                                                                  |
| Chromatography              | Mixture placed on paper, submerged in water/solvent, components move along - paper or thin layer | Stationary phase (paper) Mobil phase (solvent) | Components with greater affinity for mobile phase move further up paper than those with greater affinity for stationary phase |
 
# Paper Chromatography
- Separating and identifying pigment and other molecules from cell extracts that contain a complex mixture of molecules
- Solvent moves up paper with capillary action
	- due to attraction of solvent molecules to paper and attraction of solvent to each other
- Rf value can be compared to known values to determine pigment
	- $Rf = Distance Travelled by pigment/Distance travelled by solvent$
- RF MEANS RETARDATION
