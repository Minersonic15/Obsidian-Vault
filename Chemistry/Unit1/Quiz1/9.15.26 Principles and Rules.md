Aufbau Principle
	- Electrons added one at a time to lowest energy orbitals available until all electrons of atom are accounted for
Pauli Exclusion Principle
	- No 2 electrons in same atom can have same 4 quantum nums (same seat twice kinda)
	- Orbital hold maximum of 2 electrons (mentioned in [[9.14.26 Quantum Mechanical Wave Model]])
	- To occupy same orbital, 2 e- must spin in opposite direction
Hunds Rule
	- Electrons occupy equal energy orbitals so that maximum number of unpaired electrons results
[[9.28.26 Chemical Bonding]] has more rule
# Electron Configuration
- Used to give location of electrons in an atom
- related to 4 principal quantum numbers for each electron
- e- labelled by energy level and orbital
