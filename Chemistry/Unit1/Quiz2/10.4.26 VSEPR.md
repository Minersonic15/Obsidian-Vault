theory
- Developed in 1957
- Ronald Gillespie (canadian!)
- Predicts 3D shape of individual molecules
- Geometry of molecule determined by repulsion among pairs associated with Central atom
	- Can be bonded or unbonded
	- kinda like the graph for obsidian notes
- Only Valence electrons influence molecular shape
