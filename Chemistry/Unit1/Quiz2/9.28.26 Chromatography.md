# Paper Chromatography
- Separating and identifying pigment and other molecules from cell extracts that contain a complex mixture of molecules
- Solvent moves up paper with capillary action
	- due to attraction of solvent molecules to paper and attraction of solvent to each other
- Rf value can be compared to known values to determine pigment
	- $Rf = Distance Travelled by pigment/Distance travelled by solvent$
- RF MEANS RETARDATION
