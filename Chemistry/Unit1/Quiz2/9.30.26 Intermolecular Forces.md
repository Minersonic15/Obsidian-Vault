# Types of Molecules
Giant structures
- Bonded to each other by strong covalent bonds which form a large network
- High melting + boiling point
	- [[9.24.26 Metallic Bonding]] explains
- Mostly solid
- Diamond, sand
Molecular structures
- Few covalent bonds
	- [[9.28.26 Chemical Bonding]]
- low melting + boiling point
- Gases or low boiling liquids
- Methane, Hydrogen, chloride

# Intermolecular Forces
IMF = intermolecular force
- Forces between separate molecules and dissolved ions
- Much weaker than covalent or ionic bonds [[9.28.26 Chemical Bonding]]
- Stronger at lower temp
	- At higher temp, the kinetic energy of motion overcomes IMF
		- [[9.29.26 Intro to motion]] KINETIC ENERGY MENTIONED!!!!!
- Boiling point is a good indicator
	- stronger IMF = higher BP
	- weaker IMF = lower BP
# Van Der Waal Forces
- Random movement of electrons leading to formation of instantaneous dipole and induced dipoles in molecules
- Strength depends on molar mass of molecules
- Forces are effective over a short range
- More electrons, more chance for temp dipole
# Dipole-Dipole Forces
- in a polar-covalent bond
- Electrostatic attraction between molecs with perm dipoles
- Significantly stronger than VDW in molecules of a similar size
	- Collection of HCl molecules will align themselves such that the ve- end of one HCl molecule is attracted to +ve end of another
