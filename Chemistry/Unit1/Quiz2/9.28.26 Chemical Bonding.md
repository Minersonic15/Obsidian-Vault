# Ionic Bonding
- Complete transfer of electrons
- Involves metal and non-metal
- Forms when two oppositely charged ions are present
- Metals have low IE and low EA 
	- easily give up electrons to form cations
- Non-metals have high IE and high EA
	- They will attract electrons to form anions
- Cations attract anions to form ionic bond
- Use arrows to show transfer of electrons

# Lattice Enthalpy
- Crystal lattice structure
- Standard Enthalpy change that occurs o the formation of gaseous ions from one mole of solid lattice
- Strength of ionic bond as electrostatic forces of attraction between cations and anions must be overcome
- Endothermic

> [!hi] Lattice enthalpy increases as force of attraction increases
> Affected by ionic radius and ionic charge:
> 	increases with increasing ionic charge
> 	Decreases with increasing ionic radius

# Covalent Bonding
 [[Chemistry data Booklet]] info on page 17
Electronegativity:
- ability of an atom to attract a shared pair of e-
- Higher EN, closer e- pair will be to that atom

Non Polar Covalent bond
- Equal Sharing of electrons between two atoms
- Diatomic Molecules
	- (HOFBrINCl)
Polar Covalent bond
- Unequal sharing of electrons between two atoms
- One atom is slightly positive (lower EN) and other is slightly negative (higher EN)

# Dipoles
- Electronegativity difference of two bonded atoms
- One atom is slightly positive
- One is slightly negative
- Represented by an arrow pointing to atom with higher EN

# Octet rule
- main-group elements tend to combine so each element has eight electrons in shell
1.Odd numbers of electrons (NO)
	- Total e- = 11
	- No pairs
2. Atoms without octets (BF3)
	- B has 3 valence, therefore not enough for 4 pairs of e- around B
3. Atoms with more than an octet (SF6)
	- S has 6 pairs of e- around it

# Coordinate Bonds
- Covalent bond between two atoms where both electrons in the pair being shared have been provided by 1 atom
- Same properties as Covalent
- Achieve a stable configuration
- Donor species
	- Non-bonding electrons for bonding
- Acceptor species
	- Has space to accept electrons
