# Reactivity
- Metals
	- More reactive down a group
	- Less reactive over a period
- Non Metals
	- Less reactive down a group
	- More reactive over a period
# Atomic Radius
- Increases down a group /\ 
	- Increases from noble gas to alkali metal because new energy level is added
- Decreases across a period \\/
	- Electrostatic forces govern size of atoms
	- As num of protons in nucleus decreases, electrons more attracted and radius smaller
	- Repulsion of electrons causes radius to reduce less drastically
	- Atomic core charge(protons and inner elects) increases
# Core Charge
- Nucleus and inner electrons (not valence)
- Higher core charge = greater attraction = smaller radius

# Ionic Radius
Cations:
	- When Metal loses an electron, ion has smaller radius
		- Fewer electrons than protons, stronger attraction
		- One less energy shell
	- Down group, radius increases
		- Valence shell further away
	- Across period, radius decrease
		- More protons than electrons
Anions:
	- More repulsion when non-metals gain electrons
		- Electrons will be less attracted (same protons)
		- Radius almost doubles in size
	- Down group, size decrease
		- Valence shell further away
	- Across period, size decreases
		- Same num of electrons, but more protons
