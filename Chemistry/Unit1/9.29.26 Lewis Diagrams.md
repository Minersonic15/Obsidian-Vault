[[9.28.26 Chemical Bonding]]
Determine
- determine num of valence electrons
	- for polyatomic anions, add 1 electron for each neg charge
	- for polyatomic cations, subtract 1 electron for each positive
	- [[9.16.26 Ions]]
Draw
- Draw skeleton structure so bonding atoms are placed around central atom as far apart as possible
	- for Binary compounds (2 elecs), single atom is central atom
Count
- Count total num of electrons used and subtract num from available elecs
Place
- Place 1 pair of elecs between central atom and each of bonding atoms
Subtract
- Subtract num of electrons used from total num available
Finish
- If central atom has full set, you done
- if not, move non-bonding elec pairs from bonding to central atom (double/triple)
Formal charge
$FC = Ve^- -unbonded.e^- - bonding. e^-/2$
