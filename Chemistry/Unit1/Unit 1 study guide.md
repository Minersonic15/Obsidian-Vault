# Trends
[[9.17.26 Periodicity]]
- Atomic radius
	- Increases down a group  (top-bottom)
	- Decreases across a period (left-right)
	- Electrostatic forces govern size of atoms
- Ionic Energy
	- increases across a period (left to right)
	- decreases down a group
	- Minimum energy required to remove valence electron 
- Electronegativity
	- increases across a period
	- decreases down a group
- Electron affinity
	- decreases down a group
	- increases across a period
	- Energy to let a atom gain or lose electron

- Period 3
	- Sodium/Magnesium oxides make bases, while the nonmetal oxides make acid
